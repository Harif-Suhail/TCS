Why a web form, not the mobile app
A diver hired for three shoot days doesn't need to download an app, create an account, or sit through onboarding. They click a link, fill a form, upload an ID, sign an NDA, and they're in. The submission lands inside the Project's Talent Experience module for the producer to review — same data model as full crew, lighter intake.
